Senior design · Santa Clara University · 2026–2027

S.C.O.U.T. Reef Buoy

Santa Clara Oceanic Utilities Transmitter: a low-cost, battery-powered, modular nearshore monitoring buoy. Coral reef health is the first mission. I am the field and mechanical lead on a three-person team (mechanical, electrical, software).

Engineering and simulation

Hydrostatic stability solver

Python tool that reads the committed STEP geometry, fills it with a 3 mm voxel hull, and computes waterline, freeboard, metacentric height (GM), and the full 0–180° righting-arm curve. Checked against a plain cylinder to about 1% on draft and stiffness, then used to compare 15 float design iterations.

Interactive stability explorers

A one-page iteration explorer and a printed-float tip explorer with a heel slider, so teammates and advisors can see how each hull tips without running code.

Mass, freeboard, and load model

A written, reproducible recipe for mass, freeboard, and FEA loads, with a multi-agent verification workflow and a math-audit loop.

FEA campaign

Nine mooring load cases (uplift, current, wave, combined, overturning, 5 m hydrostatic, snap) run on two hull revisions. The results led to requiring foam fill for the 5 m pressure test. I also reviewed two hang-point FEA reports, found their setups invalid, and filed a ticket to redo them.

Design decisions

  • Float concept selection: about 15 documented iterations (snap-fit, bolted wedge, mushroom) narrowed to one printed PETG mushroom float. The ballast stem and a foam second buoy were cancelled along the way.
  • Electronics housing: sideways PG11 cable-gland deck on the cap, with a planned gland-fit coupon.
  • Hardware and coatings: 316 stainless, marine sealant, and a prototype spray coat.
  • Hang point: side through-holes with chamfers, with the rationale logged.

Test engineering and process

  • Test-record standard: printable one-page summaries with the pass mark locked before each run and a stop rule, checked automatically in CI.
  • Requirements test matrix: 24 requirements across disciplines, surfacing cross-document conflicts (for example target depth and safety factor) and gaps that needed issues filed.
  • Test campaigns: submersion tests, adhesive and O-ring failure analysis, buoy assembly, and a 4-week saltwater soak plan.

Project leadership

  • Gantt, WBS, and critical-path analysis showing the 4-week soak has no slack and must start in early November to meet the December MVP.
  • Weekly activity reports, advisor progress sheets, and Linear project structure (phases, milestones, integration goal).
